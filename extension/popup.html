FAQ & Troubleshooting

What are the quick launch hotkeys?
Press Alt+X for ERP Home, Alt+C for the CDC Notice Board, and Alt+Z for CDC Placement/Internship forms. Rebind anytime in browser shortcuts.
Gmail OTP not detecting or timing out?
If multiple Google accounts are logged in, Google can reorder slots. In Settings, click Check next to EMAIL FOR OTP to link your active mailbox.
What if ERP rejects an OTP?
Auto-login stops after 2 tries and ignores duplicate OTPs to prevent infinite loops. If it halts, click Send OTP on ERP and enter it manually.
Security question not auto-filling?
Click Fetch All (3) in Settings. ERP rotates 3 questions; once all 3 are saved, the extension answers whichever one appears on login.
Where are my credentials saved?
Stored 100% locally on your machine in Chrome's encrypted storage. Zero external servers, zero telemetry.
